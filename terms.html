Terms of play

The game

Ozymandosis is free to play. An account is needed only for online play, and you must be 13 or older to make one. Players under 16 chat with preset phrases only.

Beta

Ozymandosis is in beta. Features, balance and online services will change; ratings, match history or accounts may occasionally be reset, and there will be bugs. Memberships bought during the beta carry over.

The game and online play

Ozymandosis is free, and includes all future content. It is free software under the AGPL-3.0; the source is published at github.com/chezgoulet/ozymandosis. Single-player and play on your local network are never limited. Online play includes one full match every rolling 24 hours, free, counted when the match starts, whether you host or join, per account.

Membership

Membership makes online play unlimited. It costs $2 a month or $12 a year, tax included where tax applies, and is bought here on the website through Stripe. It renews automatically and is cancelled from your account page. A membership applies wherever you play: buying it once covers every platform your account plays on. Refunds follow Stripe’s policy.

Community rules

  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or sexual content, in names or chat.
  • No cheating, exploiting bugs against other players, or tampering with other players’ games. Players’ games check the host’s match; results that do not add up do not count and are reviewed.
  • No impersonating staff.

Moderators may warn, mute, suspend or ban accounts that break these rules. You can report players from the game.

Availability

Online services may change or pause for maintenance. Matches in progress run between players and usually continue if our servers go down.
