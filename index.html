Built to be lived in

Harvest the light that drifts through the water, grow it into organs, and decide what your creatures become: legs to hold ground, flagella to raid, mandibles to bite, pili to drink deeper. Every match is a body plan you invent and then have to defend — and when it goes wrong, the colony’s colour tells you before the numbers do.

Design your creatures

Thirty organs across legs, flagella, pili, mandibles and antennae. Combine them in the Spawnforge; every organ changes how a creature looks and fights.

A score that listens

The soundtrack is composed as you play: each culture has its own theme and mode, and the music swells from drifting ambience to pounding pulse as the fighting grows.

Readable by design

Hyperlegible type for dyslexic players, shape markers so colour is never the only signal, scalable interface, and controls for touch, mouse and keyboard.

Alive, not animated

Procedural creatures that grow the parts you evolve, a colony whose condition shows in its colour, and a world that burns, floods and grows back.

Play with up to five others. Online global multiplayer when you’re ready to spore out: one full match every 24 hours is free, and a membership makes it unlimited. Play on your local network needs no account and no internet, and always will.

Free, and open

No price, no ads, no tracking, and nothing hidden behind a store: the creatures, the codex and the music are all built from code, and the whole thing is published as free software.

Free on every platform

One full online match every 24 hours is free — hosting or joining, however long it runs. A membership makes online play unlimited, and it is bought here on the website rather than inside the app. Play on your local network is free, always, with no account and no internet.

Open source

Ozymandosis is free software under the AGPL-3.0. The game, the service it plays through and the tools that build both live in one repository — github.com/chezgoulet/ozymandosis — so anyone can read it, build it, or run a world of their own.

Where to get it

Play it in your browser at play.ozymandosis.com, or install the Android app with Obtainium, which keeps it updated straight from the releases.
